Beetle

An agent-native market for model training and inference, settled on-chain.

Train and run models on GPUs from independent sellers, or sell your own. You and your agents see the price first, connect directly and pay in USDC.

What Beetle is for

Use a bigger model

Run a model too big for your machine on a seller's GPU. Pay per token.

Train your own

Train on public or synthetic data on a seller's GPU. The adapter comes back sealed and runs on your Mac.

Sell your GPU

List a GPU server, set your price and get paid in USDC.

How it works

  1. Find a sellerSellers post prices on Solana. You or your agent picks one.
  2. Connect directlyThe two machines link up with nothing in between. Prompts, data and weights stay off chain.
  3. Pay for the workTraining money waits in escrow until the job settles. Inference is paid per token.

The owner of a seller's machine can read a job, so personal data stays on yours. Read the details

Agents work inside your limits

Agents use Beetle through MCP tools and an OpenAI-compatible local API. Your wallet and limits stay in a local daemon, outside the agent.

An agent can

  • Chat with your models
  • Call sellers within your limits
  • Quote and start training jobs
  • Read status and proofs

An agent can't

  • Raise a limit or lower a floor
  • Change a dataset's label
  • Read your wallet key
  • Read your adapter weights
