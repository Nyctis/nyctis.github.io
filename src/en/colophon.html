How this site is made

Name

Nyctis comes from Nyx, the Greek word for night. Beetle is the name of the product.

Mark

Two crescents, each turned 180 degrees from the other. Each passes over its partner at one end and under it at the other, so the two are linked and cannot separate without a cut. In Hesiod, Night and Day cross at one threshold and the house never holds them both. We apply a similar rule: the sealed result and the key to it are delivered separately.

Type and colour

IBM Plex Sans for text, IBM Plex Mono for figures, hashes and code, and IBM Plex Serif for headlines, all under the SIL Open Font License and served from this site.

Colour is mostly absent. Green, amber and red mean state. Brass marks your current position and the seal.
